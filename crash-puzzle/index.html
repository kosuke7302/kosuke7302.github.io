How to play

  • Tap a bright block to move it into the stock at the bottom. Dim blocks are blocked by blocks in front of them.
  • Three blocks of the same color in the stock disappear.
  • Clear every block to finish the stage. If all five stock slots fill up without a match, the game is over.
