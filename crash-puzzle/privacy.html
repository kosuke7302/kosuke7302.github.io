Privacy Policy

Crash Puzzle · Effective November 7, 2026

This policy explains how Crash Puzzle ("the app") handles information. The app is developed by kosuke7302 ("we").

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information. The app does not require an account. Your game progress (which stages you have cleared) is saved only on your device and is not sent to us.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect the following information from your device:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ads you have seen and your interactions with them
  • Performance and crash diagnostics

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Your choices

  • Tracking: The app asks for permission before it accesses your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland: Where required by law, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
